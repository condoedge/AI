Question asked
"Am I allowed to have two dogs?"
Orchestrator - Routing
Co-owner (standard response)
The user is a co-owner, so the request must be routed to 'base' in accordance with established rules.

Response

Hello!

Whether you can have two dogs in your condominium depends primarily on the condominium by-laws. This document sets the rules for communal living and may include provisions regarding pet ownership.

Here is what I recommend you do:

  1. Check the condominium by-laws: Review this document to see if there are any restrictions or specific conditions on pet ownership.
  2. Respect good neighbourly conduct: Even if the by-laws allow you to have two dogs, make sure their presence does not cause noise disturbances or odours for your neighbours.
  3. Precautions and legal obligations: Make sure your dogs are properly identified (microchip or tattoo) and vaccinated according to current standards. Also remember to pick up after them in common areas.

If you have specific questions or if the by-laws are unclear, do not hesitate to speak with your property manager or to contact your building's board of directors.

Feel free to come back to me if you have any other questions!

Question asked
"Can a resident at 123 Condo Street have 2 dogs?"
Orchestrator - Routing
ProximaCondo (Proximity management)
The user has the role 'ProximaCondo Management', so the request must be routed to 'team1' which handles this type of management.

Response

To determine whether a resident at 123 Condo Street can have two dogs, it is important to consult the condominium by-laws for that building. This document outlines the specific rules regarding pets, including the number of dogs allowed per unit. This can vary from one condominium to another based on decisions made by the co-ownership syndicate.

In general, here is how to proceed to obtain this information:

  1. Review the condominium by-laws: Consult the official document for the rules regarding pets.
  2. Contact the co-ownership syndicate: They will be able to confirm what the regulations state.
  3. Gather opinions or precedents: Sometimes it is possible to rely on precedents to obtain exceptions.

ProximaCondo | Our Approach

  1. Initial communication: We encourage residents to contact their dedicated manager directly to discuss their concerns. The manager can then help clarify the regulations in effect.
  2. Personalised assessment: If the rules are unclear or inadequate, we may convene a resident advisory committee to assess the situation collectively.
  3. Guidelines and mediation: In case of conflict between neighbours, our manager intervenes with proactive mediation, fostering constructive dialogue and seeking an acceptable compromise.
  4. Timeline: We commit to providing a clarification or action plan within 30 days of the issue being reported.

To determine whether a resident at 123 Condo Street can have two dogs, it is important to consult the condominium by-laws for the building. This document outlines the specific rules regarding pets.

  1. Review the condominium by-laws: Consult the official document for rules regarding pets. Some condominiums limit the number of animals or have breed restrictions.
  2. Contact the co-ownership syndicate: They will be able to confirm what the regulations state as well as any recent updates.
  3. Gather opinions or precedents: Sometimes it is possible to rely on precedents to obtain exceptions. However, this will often require formal approval from the board of directors.

StratoGestion | Analysis & Recommendations

  • Resident Satisfaction Index (RSI): Measuring the impact of pet policies on resident satisfaction can provide the data needed to re-evaluate the rules.
